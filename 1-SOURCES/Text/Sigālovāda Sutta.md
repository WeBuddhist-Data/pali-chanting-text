# Sigālovāda Sutta ^0

Evaṃ me sutaṃ, ekaṃ samayaṃ Bhagavā Rājagahe viharati Veḷuvane Kalandakanivāpe. ^0-1

Tena kho pana samayena Sigālako gahapatiputto kālasseva uṭṭhāya Rājagahā nikkhamitvā allavattho allakeso pajjaliko puthuddisā namassati—puratthimaṃ disaṃ, dakkhiṇaṃ disaṃ, pacchimaṃ disaṃ, uttaraṃ disaṃ, heṭṭhimaṃ disaṃ, uparimaṃ disaṃ. ^0-2

Atha kho Bhagavā pubbaṇhasamayaṃ nivāsetvā pattacīvaramādāya Rājagahaṃ piṇḍāya pāvisi. ^0-3

Atha kho Bhagavā Sigālakaṃ gahapatiputtaṃ kālasseva vuṭṭhāya Rājagahā nikkhamitvā allavatthaṃ allakesaṃ pajjalikaṃ puthuddisā namassantaṃ disvā Sigālakaṃ gahapatiputtaṃ etadavoca— ^0-4

“Kiṃ nu kho tvaṃ, gahapatiputta, kālasseva vuṭṭhāya Rājagahā nikkhamitvā allavattho allakeso pajjaliko puthuddisā namassasi?” ^0-5

“Pitā maṃ, bhante, kālaṃ karonto evaṃ avaca—‘Disā, tāta, namasseyyāsī’ti.” ^0-6

So kho ahaṃ, bhante, pituvacanaṃ sakkaronto garuṃ karonto mānento pūjento kālasseva vuṭṭhāya Rājagahā nikkhamitvā allavattho allakeso pajjaliko puthuddisā namassāmi. ^0-7

## Evaṃ cha disā namassitabbā ^1-0

“Na kho, gahapatiputta, ariyassa vinaye evaṃ cha disā namassitabbā”ti. ^1-1

“Yathā kathaṃ pana, bhante, ariyassa vinaye cha disā namassitabbā?” ^1-2

“Tena hi, gahapatiputta, suṇohi, sādhukaṃ manasi karohi, bhāsissāmī”ti. ^1-3

## Gahapatissa cattāro kammakilesā ^2-0

“Yato kho, gahapatiputta, ariyasāvakassa cattāro kammakilesā pahīnā honti,” ^2-1

“Catudhā ca ṭhānehi pāpakammaṃ na karoti,” ^2-2

“Cha ca bhogāni apāyamukhāni na sevati;” ^2-3

“So evaṃ chaddhā pāpakāpagato chaddisāpaṭicchādī ubholokavijjāya paṭipanno hoti.” ^2-4

“Tassa ayañceva loko āraddho hoti paro ca loko.” ^2-5

“So kāyassa bhedā paraṃ maraṇā sugatiṃ saggaṃ lokaṃ upapajjati.” ^2-6

## Channaṃ disānaṃ pūjanaṃ ^3-0

“Kathaṃ ca, gahapatiputta, ariyasāvako chaddisāpaṭicchādī hoti?” ^3-1

“Cha imā, gahapatiputta, disā veditabbā.” ^3-2

“Puratthimā disā mātāpitaro veditabbā,” ^3-3

“Dakkhiṇā disā ācariyā veditabbā,” ^3-4

“Pacchimā disā puttadārā veditabbā,” ^3-5

“Uttarā disā mittāmaccā veditabbā,” ^3-6

“Heṭṭhimā disā dāsakammakarā veditabbā,” ^3-7

“Uparimā disā samaṇabrāhmaṇā veditabbā.” ^3-8

## Mātāpitaro — puratthimā disā ^4-0

“Pañcahi kho, gahapati, ṭhānehi puttena puratthimā disā mātāpitaro paccupaṭṭhātabbā—” ^4-1

“Bhato ne bharissāmi,” ^4-2

“Kiccaṃ nesaṃ karissāmi,” ^4-3

“Kulavaṃsaṃ ṭhapessāmi,” ^4-4

“Dāyajjaṃ paṭipajjāmi,” ^4-5

“Atha vā pana petānaṃ kālakatānaṃ dakkhiṇaṃ anuppadassāmi.” ^4-6

“Imehi kho, gahapati, pañcahi ṭhānehi puttena puratthimā disā mātāpitaro paccupaṭṭhitā” ^4-7

“Pañcahi ṭhānehi puttaṃ anukampanti—” ^4-8

“Pāpā nivārenti,” ^4-9

“Kalyāṇe nivesenti,” ^4-10

“Sippaṃ sikkhāpenti,” ^4-11

“Patirūpena dārena saṃyojenti,” ^4-12

“Samaye dāyajjaṃ niyyādenti.” ^4-13

“Evaṃ assā puratthimā disā paṭicchannā hoti khemā appaṭibhayā.” ^4-14

## Ācariyā — dakkhiṇā disā ^5-0

“Pañcahi kho, gahapatiputta, ṭhānehi antevāsinā dakkhiṇā disā ācariyā paccupaṭṭhātabbā—” ^5-1

“Uṭṭhānena, upaṭṭhānena, sussūsāya, paricāriyāya, sakkaccaṃ sippapaṭiggahaṇena.” ^5-2

“Imehi kho, gahapatiputta, pañcahi ṭhānehi antevāsinā dakkhiṇā disā ācariyā paccupaṭṭhitā” ^5-3

“Pañcahi ṭhānehi antevāsiṃ anukampanti—” ^5-4

“Suvinītaṃ vinenti,” ^5-5

“Suggahitaṃ gāhāpenti,” ^5-6

“Sabbasippassa samavāyino bhavanti,” ^5-7

“MittāmAccesu paṭiyādenti,” ^5-8

“Disāsu parittāṇaṃ karonti.” ^5-9

“Evaṃ assā dakkhiṇā disā paṭicchannā hoti khemā appaṭibhayā.” ^5-10

## Bhariyā — pacchimā disā ^6-0

“Pañcahi kho, gahapatiputta, ṭhānehi sāmikena pacchimā disā bhariyā paccupaṭṭhātabbā—” ^6-1

“Sammananāya, anavamānanāya, anaticariyāya, issariyavossaggena, alaṅkārānuppadānena.” ^6-2

“Imehi kho, gahapatiputta, pañcahi ṭhānehi sāmikena pacchimā disā bhariyā paccupaṭṭhitā” ^6-3

“Pañcahi ṭhānehi anukampanti, susaṃvihitakammantā ca hoti, saṅgahitaparijanā ca, anaticārinī ca hoti, sambhataṃ ca anurakkhati.” ^6-4

“Dakkhā ca hoti analasā sabbakiccesu.” ^6-5

“Evaṃ assā pacchimā disā paṭicchannā hoti khemā appaṭibhayā.” ^6-6

## Mittāmaccā — uttarā disā ^7-0

“Pañcahi kho, gahapatiputta, ṭhānehi kulaputtena uttarā disā mittāmaccā paccupaṭṭhātabbā—” ^7-1

“Dānena, piyavajjena, atthacariyāya, samānattatāya, avisaṃvādanatāya.” ^7-2

## Dāsakammakarā — heṭṭhimā disā ^8-0

“Yathābalaṃ kammantasaṃvidhānena, bhattavetanānuppadānena, gilānupaṭṭhānena, acchariyānaṃ rasānaṃ saṃvibhāgena, samaye vossaggena.” ^8-1

“Imehi kho, gahapatiputta, pañcahi ṭhānehi ariyasāvakena heṭṭhimā disā dāsakammakarā paccupaṭṭhitā” ^8-2

“Pañcahi ṭhānehi ariyakaṃ anukampanti—” ^8-3

“Pamattam rakkhanti, pamattassa sampattiṃ rakkhanti, bhītassa saraṇaṃ honti, āpadāsu na vijahanti, aparajjaṃ tassa paṭipūjenti.” ^8-4

“Evaṃ assā heṭṭhimā disā paṭicchannā hoti khemā appaṭibhayā.” ^8-5

## Samaṇabrāhmaṇā — uparimā disā ^9-0

“Pañcahi kho, gahapatiputta, ṭhānehi kulaputtena uparimā disā samaṇabrāhmaṇā paccupaṭṭhātabbā—mettena kāya-kammena, mettena vacī-kammena, mettena mano-kammena, anāvaṭadvāratāya, āmisānuppadānena.” ^9-1

“imehi kho, gahapatiputta, pañcahi ṭhānehi kulaputtena uparimā disā samaṇabrāhmaṇā paccupaṭṭhitā chahi ṭhānehi kulaputtaṃ anukampanti—pāpā nivārenti, kalyāṇe nivesenti, kalyāṇena manasā anukampanti, asutaṃ sāventi, sutaṃ pariyādāpenti, suggatiṃ saggalokaṃ paṭipādenti. Evaṃ assā uparimā disā paṭicchannā hoti khemā appaṭibhayā.” ^9-2

## Pariniṭṭhāna Gāthā ^10-0

“Mātāpitaro disā pubbā, ācariyā dakkhiṇā disā.
Puttadārā disā pacchā, mittāmaccā ca uttarā.” ^10-1

“Dāsakammakarā heṭṭhā, uddhaṃ samaṇabrāhmaṇā.
Etā disā namasseyya, alamatto kule gihī.” ^10-2

“Paṇḍito sīlasampanno, saṇho ca paṭibhānavā.
Nivātavutti atthaddho, tādiso labhate yasaṃ.” ^10-3

“Uṭṭhānako analaso, āpadāsu na vedhati.
Acchiddavutti medhāvī, tādiso labhate yasaṃ.” ^10-4

“Saṅgāhako mittakaro, vadaññū vītamaccharo.
Netā vinetā anunetā, tādiso labhate yasaṃ.” ^10-5

“Dānañca peyyavajjañca, atthacariyā ca yā idha.
Samānattatā ca dhammesu, tattha tattha yathārahaṃ.
Ete kho saṅgahā loke, rathassāṇīva yāyato.” ^10-6

“Ete ca saṅgahā nāssu, na mātā puttakāraṇā.
Labhetha mātā puttaṃ vā, pitā vā puttakāraṇā.” ^10-7

“Yasmā ca saṅgahā ete, samavekkhanti paṇḍitā.
Tasmā mahattaṃ papponti, pāsaṃsā ca bhavanti te ti.” ^10-8

## Sigālakassa Saraṇagamanaṃ ^11-0

“Abhikkantaṃ, bhante, abhikkantaṃ, bhante! ... Esāhaṃ, bhante, Bhagavantaṃ saraṇaṃ gacchāmi, Dhammañca bhikkhusaṅghañca. Upāsakaṃ maṃ Bhagavā dhāretu, ajjatagge pāṇupetaṃ saraṇaṃ gataṃ.” ^11-1
