# Dāna Suttaṃ ^0

Ekaṃ samayaṃ Bhagavā Sāvatthiyaṃ viharati Jetavane Anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme. ^0-1

Tena kho pana samayena Veḷukaṇḍakī Nandamātā upāsikā Sāriputtamoggallānapamukhe bhikkhusaṅghe chaḷaṅgasamannāgataṃ dakkhiṇaṃ patiṭṭhāpeti. ^0-2

Addasā kho Bhagavā dibbena cakkhunā visuddhena atikkantamānusakena Veḷukaṇḍakiṃ Nandamātaraṃ upāsikaṃ Sāriputtamoggallānapamukhe bhikkhusaṅghe chaḷaṅgasamannāgataṃ dakkhiṇaṃ patiṭṭhāpentiṃ. ^0-3

Disvā bhikkhū āmantesi— ^0-4

‘Esā, bhikkhave, Veḷukaṇḍakī Nandamātā upāsikā Sāriputtamoggallānapamukhe bhikkhusaṅghe chaḷaṅgasamannāgataṃ dakkhiṇaṃ patiṭṭhāpeti.’ ^0-5

Kathañca, bhikkhave, chaḷaṅgasamannāgatā dakkhiṇā hoti? ^0-6

Idha, bhikkhave, dāyakassa tīṇi aṅgāni honti, paṭiggāhakānaṃ tīṇi aṅgāni. ^0-7

Katamāni dāyakassa tīṇi aṅgāni? ^0-8

Idha, bhikkhave, dāyako pubbeva dānā sumano hoti, ^0-9

Dadaṃ cittaṃ pasādeti, ^0-10

Datvā attamano hoti. ^0-11

Imāni dāyakassa tīṇi aṅgāni. ^0-12

Katamāni paṭiggāhakānaṃ tīṇi aṅgāni? ^0-13

Idha, bhikkhave, paṭiggāhakā vītarāgā vā honti rāgavinayāya vā paṭipannā, ^0-14

Vītadosā vā honti dosavinayāya vā paṭipannā, ^0-15

Vītamohā vā honti mohavinayāya vā paṭipannā. ^0-16

Imāni paṭiggāhakānaṃ tīṇi aṅgāni. ^0-17

Iti dāyakassa tīṇi aṅgāni, paṭiggāhakānaṃ tīṇi aṅgāni. ^0-18

Evaṃ kho, bhikkhave, chaḷaṅgasamannāgatā dakkhiṇā hoti. ^0-19

Evaṃ chaḷaṅgasamannāgatāya, bhikkhave, dakkhiṇāya na sukaraṃ puññassa pamāṇaṃ gahetuṃ— ^0-20

‘Ettako puññābhisando kusalābhisando sukhassāhāro sovaggiko sukhavipāko saggasaṃvattaniko iṭṭhāya kantāya manāpāya hitāya sukhāya saṃvattaniko’ti. ^0-21

Atha kho asaṅkheyyo appameyyo mahāpuññakkhandho tveva saṅkhaṃ gacchati. ^0-22

Pubbeva dānā sumano, dadaṃ cittaṃ pasādaye. ^0-23

Datvā attamano hoti, esā yaññassa sampadā. ^0-24

Vītarāgā vītadosā, vītamohā anāsavā. ^0-25

Khettaṃ yaññassa sampannaṃ, saññatā brahmacārayo. ^0-26

Sayaṃ ācamayitvāna, datvā sakehi pāṇibhi. ^0-27

Attano parato ceso, yañño hoti mahappalo. ^0-28

Evaṃ yajitvā medhāvī, suddho mutte cittasā. ^0-29

Abyāpajjaṃ sukhaṃ lokaṃ, paṇḍito upapajjatīti. ^0-30
