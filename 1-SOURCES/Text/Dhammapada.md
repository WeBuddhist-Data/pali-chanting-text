# Dhammapada ^0

## Yamakavagga ^1-0

manopubbaṅgamā dhammā manoseṭṭhā manomayā,
manasā ce paduṭṭhena bhāsatī vā karoti vā
tato naṃ dukkham anveti cakkaṃ va vahato padaṃ. ^1-1

manopubbaṅgamā dhammā manoseṭṭhā manomayā,
manasā ce pasannena bhāsatī vā karoti vā
tato naṃ sukham anveti chāyā va anapāyinī. ^1-2

"akkocchi maṃ avadhi maṃ ajini maṃ ahāsi me",
ye taṃ upanayihanti veraṃ tesaṃ na sammati. ^1-3

"akkocchi maṃ avadhi maṃ ajini maṃ ahāsi me",
ye taṃ na upanayhanti veraṃ tes' ūpasammati. ^1-4

na hi verena verāni sammant' idha kudācanaṃ
averena ca sammanti, esa dhammo sanantano. ^1-5

pare ca na vijānanti: "mayam ettha yamāmase",
ye ca tattha vijānanti tato sammanti medhagā. ^1-6

subhānupassiṃ viharantaṃ indriyesu asaṃvutaṃ
bhojanamhi câmattaññuṃ kusītaṃ hīnavīriyaṃ
taṃ ve pasahatī Māro vāto rukkhaṃ va dubbalaṃ. ^1-7

asubhānupassiṃ viharantaṃ indriyesu susaṃvutaṃ
bhojanamhi ca mattaññuṃ saddhaṃ āraddhavīriyaṃ
taṃ [ve] na-ppasahatī Māro vāto selaṃ va pabbataṃ. ^1-8

anikkasāvo kāsāvaṃ yo vatthaṃ paridahessati
apeto damasaccena na so kāsāvaṃ arhati. ^1-9

yo ca vantakasāv' assa sīlesu susamāhito
upeto damasaccena sa ve kāsāvam arhati. ^1-10

asāre sāramatino sāre câsāradassino
te sāraṃ nâdhigacchanti micchāsaṃkappagocarā. ^1-11

sārañ ca sārato ñatvā asārañ ca asārato
te sāraṃ adhigacchanti sammāsaṃkappagocarā. ^1-12

yathā agāraṃ ducchannaṃ vuṭṭhi samativijjhati
evaṃ abhāvitaṃ cittaṃ rāgo samativijjhati. ^1-13

yathā agāraṃ succhannaṃ vuṭṭhi na samativijjhati
evaṃ subhāvitaṃ cittaṃ rāgo na samativijjhati. ^1-14

idha socati pecca socati pāpakārī ubhayattha socati,
so socati so vihaññati disvā kammakiliṭṭham attano. ^1-15

idha modati pecca modati katapuñño ubhayattha modati,
so modati so pamodati disvā kammavisuddhim attano. ^1-16

idha tappati pecca tappati pāpakārī ubhayattha tappati,
"pāpaṃ me katan" ti tappati. bhiyyo tappati duggatiṃ gato. ^1-17

idha nandati pecca nandati katapuñño, ubhayattha nandati,
"puññam me katan" ti nandati. bhiyyo nandati suggatiṃ gato. ^1-18

bahum pi ce sahitam bhāsamāno na takkaro hoti naro pamatto
gopo va gāvo gaṇayam paresaṃ na bhāgavā sāmaññassa hoti. ^1-19

appam pi ce sahitam bhāsamāno dhammassa hoti anudhammacārī
rāgañ ca dosañ ca pahāya mohaṃ sammappajāno suvimuttacitto
anupādiyāno idha vā huraṃ vā sa bhāgavā sāmaññassa hoti. ^1-20

## Appamādavagga ^2-0

appamādo amatapadaṃ pamādo maccuno padaṃ,
appamattā na mīyanti ye pamattā yathā matā. ^1-21

etaṃ visesato ñatvā appamādamhi paṇḍitā
appamāde pamodanti aryānaṃ gocare ratā. ^1-22

te jhāyino sātatikā niccaṃ daḷhaparakkamā
phusanti dhīrā nibbānaṃ yogakkhemaṃ anuttaraṃ. ^1-23

uṭṭhānavato satīmato sucikammassa nisammakārino
saññatassa ca dhammajīvino appamattassa yaso 'bhivaḍḍhati. ^1-24

uṭṭhānen' appamādena saññamena damena ca
dīpaṃ kayrātha medhāvī yaṃ ogho nâbhikīrati. ^1-25

pamādam anuyuñjanti bālā dummedhino janā
appamādañ ca medhāvī dhanaṃ seṭṭhaṃ va rakkhati. ^1-26

mā pamādam anuyuñjetha mā kāmaratisanthavaṃ,
appamatto hi jhāyanto pappoti vipulaṃ sukhaṃ. ^1-27

pamādam appamādena yadā nudati paṇḍito
paññāpāsādam āruyha asoko sokiniṃ pajaṃ
pabbataṭṭho va bhummaṭṭhe dhīro bāle avekkhati. ^1-28

appamatto pamattesu suttesu bahujāgaro
abalassaṃ va sīghasso hitvā yāti sumedhaso. ^1-29

appamādena Maghavā devānaṃ seṭṭhataṃ gato,
appamādaṃ pasaṃsanti pamādo garhito sadā. ^1-30

appamādarato bhikkhu pamāde bhayadassivā
saññojanaṃ aṇuṃthūlaṃ ḍahaṃ aggîva gacchati. ^1-31

appamādarato bhikkhu pamāde bhayadassivā
abhabbo parihānāya nibbānass'eva santike. ^1-32

## Cittavagga ^3-0

phandanaṃ capalaṃ cittaṃ
dūrakkhaṃ dunnivārayaṃ
ujuṃ karoti medhāvī usukāro va tejanaṃ. ^1-33

vārijo va thale khitto okamokata ubbhato
pariphandat' idaṃ cittaṃ Māradheyyaṃ pahātave. ^1-34

dunniggahassa lahuno yatthakāmanipātino
cittassa damatho sādhu, cittaṃ dantaṃ sukhāvahaṃ. ^1-35

sududdasaṃ sunipuṇaṃ yatthakāmanipātinaṃ
cittaṃ rakkhetha medhāvī, cittaṃ guttaṃ sukhāvahaṃ. ^1-36

dūraṅgamaṃ ekacaraṃ asarīraṃ guhāsayaṃ
ye cittaṃ saññamessanti mokkhanti Mārabhandhanā. ^1-37

anavaṭṭhitacittassa saddhammaṃ avijānato
pariplavapasādassa paññā na paripūrati. ^1-38

anavassutacittassa ananvāhatacetaso
puññapāpapahīnassa n' atthi jāgarato bhayaṃ. ^1-39

kumbhūpamaṃ kāyam imaṃ viditvā
nagarūpamaṃ cittam idaṃ ṭhapetvā
yojetha Māraṃ paññāvudhena
jitañ ca rakkhe anivesano siyā. ^1-40

aciraṃ vat' ayaṃ kāyo paṭhaviṃ adhisessati
chuddho apetaviññāṇo niratthaṃ va kaliṅgaraṃ. ^1-41

diso disaṃ yan taṃ kayrā verīvā pana verinaṃ -
micchāpaṇihitaṃ cittaṃ pāpiyo naṃ tato kare. ^1-42

na taṃ mātā pitā kayrā aññe vâpi ca ñātakā -
sammāpaṇihitaṃ cittaṃ seyyaso naṃ tato kare. ^1-43

## Pupphavagga ^4-0

ko imaṃ paṭhaviṃ vijessati
Yamalokañ ca imaṃ sadevakaṃ?
ko dhammapadaṃ sudesitaṃ
kusalo puppham iva-ppacessati? ^1-44

sekho paṭhaviṃ vijessati
Yamalokañ ca imaṃ sadevakaṃ.
sekho dhammapadaṃ sudesitaṃ
kusalo puppham iva-ppacessati. ^1-45

pheṇūpamaṃ kāyam imaṃ viditvā
marīcidhammaṃ abhisambudhāno
chetvāna Mārassa papupphakāni
adassanaṃ Maccurājassa gacche. ^1-46

pupphān' eva pacinantaṃ vyāsattamanasaṃ naraṃ
suttaṃ gāmaṃ mahogho va Maccu ādāya gacchati. ^1-47

pupphān' eva pacinantaṃ vyāsattamanasaṃ naraṃ
atittaṃ yeva kāmesu Antako kurute vasaṃ. ^1-48

yathâpi bhamaro pupphaṃ
vaṇṇagandhaṃ aheṭhayaṃ
paleti rasam ādāya
evaṃ gāme munī care. ^1-49

na paresaṃ vilomāni, na paresaṃ katākataṃ
attano va avekkheyya katāni akatāni ca. ^1-50

yathâpi ruciraṃ pupphaṃ vaṇṇavantaṃ agandhakaṃ
evaṃ subhāsitā vācā aphalā hot' akubbato. ^1-51

yathâpi ruciraṃ pupphaṃ vaṇṇavantaṃ sagandhakaṃ
evaṃ subhāsitā vācā saphalā hoti kubbato. ^1-52

yathâpi puppharāsimhā kayrā mālāguṇe bahū
evaṃ jātena maccena kattabbaṃ kusalaṃ bahuṃ. ^1-53

na pupphagandho paṭivātam eti
na candanaṃ tagaramallikā vā
satañ ca gandho paṭivātam eti
sabbā disā sappuriso pavāti. ^1-54

candanaṃ tagaraṃ vâpi uppalaṃ atha vassikī
etesaṃ gandhajātānaṃ sīlagandho anuttaro. ^1-55

appamatto ayaṃ gandho y'āyaṃ tagaracandanī
yo ca sīlavataṃ gandho vāti devesu uttamo. ^1-56

tesaṃ sampannasīlānaṃ appamādavihārinaṃ
sammadaññāvimuttānaṃ Māro maggaṃ na vindati. ^1-57

yathā saṃkāradhānasmiṃ ujjhitasmiṃ mahāpathe
padumaṃ tattha jāyetha sucigandhaṃ manoramaṃ; ^1-58

evaṃ saṃkārabhūtesu andhabhūte puthujjane
atirocati paññāya sammāsambuddhasāvako. ^1-59

## Bālavagga ^5-0

dīghā jāgarato rattī dīghaṃ santassa yojanaṃ
dīgho bālānaṃ saṃsāro saddhammaṃ avijānataṃ. ^1-60

carañ ce nādhigaccheyya seyyaṃ sadisam attano
ekacariyaṃ daḷhaṃ kayrā n' atthi bāle sahāyatā. ^1-61

"puttā m' atthi dhanaṃ m' atthi" iti bālo vihaññati
attā hi attano n' atthi kuto puttā kuto dhanaṃ. ^1-62

yo bālo maññatī balyaṃ paṇḍito vāpi tena so,
bālo ca paṇḍitamānī sa ve bālo ti vuccati. ^1-63

yāvajīvam pi ce bālo paṇḍitaṃ payrupāsati
na so dhammaṃ vijānāti dabbī sūparasaṃ yathā. ^1-64

muhuttam api ce viññū paṇḍitaṃ payrupāsati
khippaṃ dhammaṃ vijānāti jivhā sūparasaṃ yathā. ^1-65

caranti bālā dummedhā amittenêva attanā
karontā pāpakaṃ kammaṃ yaṃ hoti kaṭukapphalaṃ. ^1-66

na taṃ kammaṃ kataṃ sādhu yaṃ katvā anutappati
yassa assumukho rodaṃ vipākaṃ paṭisevati. ^1-67

tañ ca kammaṃ kataṃ sādhu yaṃ katvā nânutappati
yassa patīto sumano vipākaṃ paṭisevati. ^1-68

mādhuvā maññatī bālo yāva pāpaṃ na paccati
yadā ca paccatī pāpaṃ atha [bālo] dukkhaṃ nigacchati. ^1-69

māse māse kusaggena bālo bhuñjetha bhojanaṃ
na so saṃkhatadhammānaṃ kalaṃ nâgghati soḷasiṃ. ^1-70

na hi pāpaṃ kataṃ kammaṃ sajju khīraṃ va mucchati
ḍahantam bālam anveti bhasmācchanno va pāvako. ^1-71

yāvad eva anatthāya ñattaṃ bālassa jāyati
hanti bālassa sukkaṃsaṃ muddham assa vipātayaṃ. ^1-72

asataṃ bhāvan' iccheyya purekkhārañ ca bhikkhusu
āvāsesu ca issariyaṃ pūjā parakulesu ca. ^1-73

"mam' eva kata maññantu gihī pabbajitā ubho,
mam' evâtivasā assu kiccākiccesu kismici",
iti bālassa saṃkappo, icchā māno ca vaḍḍhati. ^1-74

aññā hi lābhūpanisā aññā nibbānagāminī,
evam etaṃ abhiññāya bhikkhu Buddhassa sāvako
sakkāraṃ nâbhinandeyya vivekam anubrūhaye. ^1-75

Bālavagga pañcamo ^a-5

## Paṇḍitavagga ^6-0

nidhīnaṃ va pavattāraṃ yaṃ passe vajjadassinaṃ
niggayhavādiṃ medhāviṃ tādisaṃ paṇḍitaṃ bhaje,
tādisaṃ bhajamānassa seyyo hoti na pāpiyo. ^1-76

ovadeyyânusāseyya asabbhā ca nivāraye,
sataṃ hi so piyo hoti asataṃ hoti appiyo. ^1-77

na bhaje pāpake mitte na bhaje purisādhame,
bhajetha mitte kalyāṇe bhajetha purisuttame. ^1-78

dhammapītī sukhaṃ seti vipasannena cetasā,
aryappavedite dhamme sadā ramati paṇḍito. ^1-79

udakaṃ hi nayanti nettikā
usukārā namayanti tejanaṃ
dāruṃ namayanti tacchakā
attānaṃ damayanti paṇḍitā. ^1-80

selo yathā ekaghano vātena na samīrati
evaṃ nindāpasaṃsāsu na samiñjanti paṇḍitā. ^1-81

yathâpi rhado gambhīro vippasanno anāvilo
evaṃ dhammāni sutvāna vippasīdanti paṇḍitā. ^1-82

sabbattha ve sappurisā vajanti,
na kāmakāmā lapayanti santo,
sukhena phuṭṭhā athavā dukhena
n' uccāvacaṃ paṇḍitā dassayanti. ^1-83

na attahetu na parassahetu
na puttam icche na dhanaṃ na raṭṭhaṃ
n' iccheyy' adhammena samiddhim attano
sa sīlavā paññavā dhammiko siyā. ^1-84

appakā te manussesu ye janā pāragāmino,
athâyaṃ itarā pajā tīram evânudhāvati. ^1-85

ye ca kho sammadakkhāte dhamme dhammānuvattino
te janā pāram essanti, Maccudheyyaṃ suduttaraṃ. ^1-86

kaṇhaṃ dhammaṃ vippahāya
sukkaṃ bhāvetha paṇḍito,
okā anokaṃ āgamma
viveke yattha dūramaṃ. ^1-87

tatrâbhiratim iccheyya, hitvā kāme akiñcano
pariyodapeyya attānaṃ cittaklesehi paṇḍito. ^1-88

yesaṃ sambodhiaṅgesu sammā cittaṃ subhāvitaṃ
ādānapaṭinissagge anupādāya ye ratā
khīṇāsavā jutīmanto te loke parinibbutā. ^1-89

Paṇḍitavaggo chaṭṭho ^a-6

## Arahantavagga ^7-0

gataddhino visokassa vippamuttassa sabbadhi
sabbaganthappahīnassa pariḷāho na vijjati. ^1-90

uyyuñjanti satīmanto na nikete ramanti te
haṃsā va pallalam hitvā okamokaṃ jahanti te. ^1-91

yesaṃ sannicayo n' atthi ye pariññātabhojanā
suññato animitto ca vimokho yesaṃ gocaro
ākāse va sakuntānaṃ gati tesaṃ durannayā. ^1-92

yassâsavā parikkhīṇā āhāre ca anissito
suññato animitto ca vimokho yassa gocaro
ākāse va sakuntānaṃ padaṃ tassa durannayaṃ. ^1-93

yass' indriyāni samathaṃ gatāni
assā yathā sārathinā sudantā
pahīnamānassa anāsavassa
devâpi tassa pihayanti tādino. ^1-94

paṭhavīsamo no virujjhati
indakhīlūpamo tādi subbato
rahado va apetakaddamo
saṃsārā na bhavanti tādino. ^1-95

santaṃ tassa manaṃ hoti santā vācā ca kamma ca
sammadaññāvimuttassa upasantassa tādino. ^1-96

assaddho akataññū ca sandhicchedo ca yo naro
hatāvakāso vantāso sa ve uttamaporiso. ^1-97

gāme vā yadivâraññe ninne vā yadivā thale
yatth' arahanto viharanti taṃ bhūmiṃrāmaṇeyyakaṃ. ^1-98

ramaṇīyān' araññāni, yattha na ramatī jano
vītarāgā ramissanti, na te kāmagavesino. ^1-99

## Sahassavagga ^8-0

sahassam api ce vācā anatthapadasaṃhitā
ekam atthapadaṃ seyyo yaṃ sutvā upasammati. ^1-100

sahassam api ce gāthā anatthapadasaṃhitā
ekaṃ gāthāpadaṃ seyyo yaṃ sutvā upasammati. ^1-101

yo ca gāthā sataṃ bhāse anatthapadasaṃhitā
ekaṃ dhammapadaṃ seyyo yaṃ sutvā upasammati. ^1-102

yo sahassaṃ sahassena saṅgāme mānuse jine
ekañ ca jeyya-m-attānaṃ sa ve saṅgāmajuttamo. ^1-103

attā have jitaṃ seyyo yā c' āyaṃ itarā pajā,
attadantassa posassa niccaṃ saññatacārino. ^1-104

n' eva devo na gandhabbo na Māro saha Brahmunā
jitaṃ apajitaṃ kayrā tathārūpassa jantuno. ^1-105

māse māse sahassena yo yajetha sataṃsamaṃ
ekañ ca bhāvitattānaṃ muhuttam api pūjaye -
sā yeva pūjanā seyyo yañ ce vassasataṃ hutaṃ. ^1-106

yo ca vassasataṃ jantu aggiṃ paricare vane
ekañ ca bhāvitattānaṃ muhuttam api pūjaye -
sā yeva pūjanā seyyo yañ ce vassasataṃ hutaṃ. ^1-107

yaṃ kiñci yiṭṭhañ ca hutañ ca loke
saṃvaccharaṃ yajetha puññapekho
sabbam pi taṃ na catubhāgam eti, -
abhivādanā ujjugatesu seyyo. ^1-108

abhivādanasīlissa niccaṃ vaddhāpacāyino
cattāro dhammā vaḍḍhanti: āyu vaṇṇo sukhaṃ balaṃ. ^1-109

yo ca vassasataṃ jīve dussīlo asamāhito -
ekāhaṃ jīvitaṃ seyyo sīlavantassa jhāyino. ^1-110

yo ca vassasataṃ jīve duppañño asamāhito -
ekāhaṃ jīvitaṃ seyyo paññāvantassa jhāyino. ^1-111

yo ca vassasataṃ jīve kusīto hīnavīriyo
ekāhaṃ jīvitaṃ seyyo vīryam ārabhato daḷhaṃ. ^1-112

yo ca vassasataṃ jīve apassaṃ udayavyayaṃ -
ekāhaṃ jīvitaṃ seyyo passato udayavyayaṃ. ^1-113

yo ca vassasataṃ jīve apassaṃ amataṃ padaṃ -
ekāhaṃ jīvitaṃ seyyo passato amataṃ padaṃ. ^1-114

yo ca vassasataṃ jīve apassaṃ dhammam uttamaṃ -
ekāhaṃ jīvitaṃ seyyo passato dhammaṃ uttamam. ^1-115

## Pāpavagga ^9-0

abhittharetha kalyāṇe pāpā cittaṃ nivāraye,
dandhaṃ hi karoto puññaṃ pāpasmiṃ ramatī mano. ^1-116

pāpañ ce pūriso kayrā na taṃ kayrā punappunaṃ,
na tamhi chandaṃ kayrātha, dukkho pāpassa uccayo. ^1-117

puññañ ce pūriso kayrā kayrāth' enaṃ punappunaṃ,
tamhi chandaṃ kayirātha, sukho puññassa uccayo. ^1-118

pāpo pi passatī bhadraṃ yāva pāpaṃ na paccati,
yadā ca paccatī pāpaṃ atha [pāpo] pāpāni passati. ^1-119

bhadro pi passatī pāpaṃ
yāva bhadraṃ na paccati,
yadā ca paccatī bhadraṃ
atha [bhadro] bhadrāni passati. ^1-120

mâppamaññetha pāpassa "na man taṃ āgamissati".
udabindunipātena udakumbho pi pūrati.
bālo pūrati pāpassa thokathokam pi ācinaṃ. ^1-121

mâppamaññetha puññassa "na man taṃ āgamissati",
udabindunipātena udakumbho pi pūrati,
dhīro pūrati puññassa thokathokam pi ācinaṃ. ^1-122

vāṇijo va bhayaṃ maggaṃ appasattho mahaddhano
visaṃ jīvitukāmo va pāpāni parivajjaye. ^1-123

pāṇimhi ce vaṇo nâssa hareyya pāṇinā visaṃ,

## Brāhmaṇavagga ^26-0

chinda sotaṃ parakkamma, kāme panuda brāhmaṇa,
saṃkhārānaṃ khayaṃ ñatvā akataññū si brāhmaṇa. ^1-383

yadā dvayesu dhammesu pāragū hoti brāhmaṇo
ath' assa sabbe saṃyogā atthaṃ gacchanti jānato. ^1-384

yassa pāraṃ apāraṃ vā pārāpāraṃ na vijjati
vītaddaraṃ visaññuttaṃ tam ahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ. ^1-385

jhāyiṃ virajam āsīnaṃ katakiccaṃ anāsavaṃ
uttamatthaṃ anuppattaṃ tam ahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ. ^1-386

divā tapati ādicco, rattiṃ ābhāti candimā.
sannaddho khattyo tapati, jhāyī tapati brāhmaṇo.
atha sabbam ahorattiṃ Buddho tapati tejasā. ^1-387

bāhitapāpo ti brāhmaṇo
samacaryā samaṇo ti vuccati.
pabbājayam attano malaṃ
tasmā pabbajito ti vuccati. ^1-388

na brāhmaṇassa hareyya;
nâssa muñcetha brāhmaṇo,
dhī brāhmaṇassa hantāraṃ,
tato dhī y' assa muñcati. ^1-389

na brāhmaṇass' etad akiñci seyyo
yadā nisedho manaso piyehi,
yato yato hiṃsamano nivattati
tato tato sammati-m-eva dukkhaṃ. ^1-390

yassa kāyena vācāya manasā n' atthi dukkataṃ
saṃvutaṃ tīhi ṭhānehi tam ahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ. ^1-391

yamhā dhammaṃ vijāneyya
sammāsambuddhadesitaṃ
sakkaccaṃ taṃ namasseyya
aggihuttaṃ va brāhmaṇo. ^1-392

na jaṭāhi na gottena
na jaccā hoti brāhmaṇo,
yamhi saccañ ca dhammo ca
so sukhī so ca brāhmaṇo. ^1-393

kin te jaṭāhi dummedha, kin te ajinasāṭiyā.
abbhantaran te gahanaṃ, bāhiraṃ parimajjasi. ^1-394

paṃsukūladharaṃ jantuṃ
kisaṃ dhamanisanthataṃ
ekaṃ vanasmiṃ jhāyantaṃ
tam ahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ. ^1-395

na câhaṃ brāhmaṇaṃ brūmi
yonijaṃ mattisambhavaṃ
bhovādi nāma so hoti
sa ve hoti sakiñcano,
akiñcanaṃ anādānaṃ
tam ahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ. ^1-396

sabbasaṃyojanaṃ chetvā yo ve na paritassati
saṅgātigaṃ visaṃyuttaṃ tam ahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇam. ^1-397

chetvā nandhiṃ varattañ ca
sandānaṃ sahanukkamaṃ
ukkhittapaḷighaṃ buddhaṃ
tam ahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ. ^1-398

akkosaṃ vadhabandhañ ca aduṭṭho yo titikkhati
khantībalaṃ balānīkaṃ tam ahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ. ^1-399

akkodhanaṃ vatavantaṃ sīlavantaṃ anussutaṃ
dantaṃ antimasārīraṃ tam ahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ. ^1-400

vāri pokkharapatte va āragge-r-iva sāsapo
yo na lippati kāmesu tam ahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ. ^1-401

yo dukkhassa pajānāti
idh' eva khayam attano
pannabhāraṃ visaññuttaṃ
tam ahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ. ^1-402

gambhīrapaññaṃ medhāviṃ
maggāmaggassa kovidaṃ
uttamatthaṃ anuppattaṃ
tam ahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ. ^1-403

asaṃsaṭṭhaṃ gahaṭṭhehi anāgārehi c' ūbhayaṃ
anokasāriṃ appicchaṃ tam - ^1-404

nidhāya daṇḍaṃ bhūtesu tasesu thāvaresu ca
yo na hanti na ghāteti tam - ^1-405

aviruddhaṃ viruddhesu attadaṇḍesu nibbutaṃ
sādānesu anādānaṃ tam - ^1-406

yassa rāgo ca doso ca māno makkho ca pātito
sāsapo-r-iva āraggā tam - ^1-407

akakkasaṃ viññāpaniṃ giraṃ saccaṃ udīraye
yāya nâbhisaje kañci tam - ^1-408

yo 'dha dīghaṃ va rassaṃ vā aṇuṃthūlaṃ subhāsubhaṃ
loke adinnaṃ nâdiyate tam - ^1-409

āsā yassa na vijjanti asmiṃ loke paramhi ca
nirāsayaṃ visaṃyuttaṃ tam - ^1-410

yassâlayā na vijjanti aññāya akathaṃkathī
amatogadhaṃ anuppattaṃ tam - ^1-411

yo 'dha puññañ ca pāpañ ca ubho saṅgaṃ upaccagā
asokaṃ virajaṃ suddhaṃ tam - ^1-412

candaṃ va vimalaṃ suddhaṃ vippasannam anāvilaṃ
nandībhavaparikkhīṇaṃ tam - ^1-413

yo' maṃ palipathaṃ duggaṃ saṃsāraṃ moham accagā
tiṇṇo pāragato jhāyī anejo akathaṃkathī
anupādāya nibbuto tam - ^1-414

yo 'dha kāme pahatvāna anāgāro paribbaje
kāmābhavaparikkhīṇaṃ tam - ^1-415

yo 'dha taṇhaṃ pahatvāna anāgāro paribbaje
taṇhābhavaparikkhīṇaṃ tam - ^1-416

hitvā mānusakaṃ yogaṃ dibbaṃ yogaṃ upaccagā
sabbayogavisaṃyuttaṃ tam - ^1-417

hitvā ratiñ câratiñ ca sītibhūtaṃ nirūpadhiṃ
sabbalokābhibhuṃ vīraṃ tam - ^1-418

cutiṃ yo vedi sattānaṃ upapattiñ ca sabbaso
asattaṃ sugataṃ buddhaṃ tam - ^1-419

yassa gatiṃ na jānanti devā gandhabbamānusā
khīṇāsavaṃ arahantaṃ tam - ^1-420

yassa pure ca pacchā ca majjhe ca n' atthi kiñcanaṃ
akiñcanaṃ anādānaṃ tam - ^1-421

usabhaṃ pavaraṃ vīraṃ mahesiṃ vijitāvinaṃ
anejaṃ nhātakaṃ buddhaṃ tam - ^1-422

pubbenivāsaṃ yo vedi saggāpāyañ ca passati
atho jātikkhayaṃ patto abhiññāvosito muni
sabbavositavosānaṃ tam - ^1-423

Brāhmaṇavaggo chabbīsatimo ^a-27

yamakaṃ appamādaṃ cittaṃ
pupphaṃ bālena paṇḍitaṃ
arahantaṃ sahassena
pāpaṃ daṇḍena, te dasa. ^a-28

jarā attā ca loko ca buddhaṃ sukhaṃ piyena ca
kodhaṃ malañ ca dhammaṭṭhaṃ maggavaggena vīsati. ^a-29

pakiṇṇaṃ nirayaṃ nāgo
taṇhaṃ bhikkhu ca brāhmaṇo,
ete chabbīsatī vaggā
desitâdiccabandhunā. ^a-30

Dhammapadaṃ niṭṭhitaṃ ^a-31
