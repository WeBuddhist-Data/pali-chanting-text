# Vitthatadhana Suttaṃ ^0

Sattimāni, bhikkhave, dhanāni. ^0-1

Katamāni satta? ^0-2

Saddhādhanaṃ, sīladhanaṃ, hiridhanaṃ, ottappadhanaṃ, sutadhanaṃ, cāgadhanaṃ, paññādhanaṃ. ^0-3

Katamaṃ ca, bhikkhave, saddhādhanaṃ? ^0-4

Idha, bhikkhave, ariyasāvako saddho hoti. ^0-5

Saddahati Tathāgatassa bodhiṃ— ^0-6

‘Iti pi so Bhagavā arahaṃ sammāsambuddho vijjācaraṇasampanno, ^0-7

Sugato lokavidū anuttaro purisadammasārathi satthā devamanussānaṃ buddho Bhagavā’ti. ^0-8

Idaṃ vuccati, bhikkhave, saddhādhanaṃ. ^0-9

Katamaṃ ca, bhikkhave, sīladhanaṃ? ^0-10

Idha, bhikkhave, ariyasāvako pāṇātipātā paṭivirato hoti, ^0-11

Adinnādānā paṭivirato hoti, ^0-12

Kāmesumicchācārā paṭivirato hoti, ^0-13

Musāvādā paṭivirato hoti, ^0-14

Surāmerayamajjapamādaṭṭhānā paṭivirato hoti. ^0-15

Idaṃ vuccati, bhikkhave, sīladhanaṃ. ^0-16

Katamaṃ ca, bhikkhave, hiridhanaṃ? ^0-17

Idha, bhikkhave, ariyasāvako hirīmā hoti. ^0-18

Hiriyati kāyaduccaritena, vacīduccaritena, manoduccaritena, ^0-19

Hiriyati pāpakānaṃ akusalānaṃ dhammānaṃ samāpattiyā. ^0-20

Idaṃ vuccati, bhikkhave, hiridhanaṃ. ^0-21

Katamaṃ ca, bhikkhave, ottappadhanaṃ? ^0-22

Idha, bhikkhave, ariyasāvako ottappī hoti, ^0-23

Ottappati kāyaduccaritena, vacīduccaritena, manoduccaritena, ^0-24

Ottappati pāpakānaṃ akusalānaṃ dhammānaṃ samāpattiyā. ^0-25

Idaṃ vuccati, bhikkhave, ottappadhanaṃ. ^0-26

Katamaṃ ca, bhikkhave, sutadhanaṃ? ^0-27

Idha, bhikkhave, ariyasāvako bahussuto hoti sutadharo sutasannicayo. ^0-28

Ye te dhammā ādikalyāṇā majjhekalyāṇā pariyosānakalyāṇā ^0-29

Sātthaṃ sabyañjanaṃ, kevalaparipuṇṇaṃ parisuddhaṃ brahmacariyaṃ abhivadanti, ^0-30

Tathārūpāssa dhammā bahussutā honti dhātā vacasā paricitā manasānupakkhitā diṭṭhiyā suppaṭividdhā. ^0-31

Idaṃ vuccati, bhikkhave, sutadhanaṃ. ^0-32

Katamaṃ ca, bhikkhave, cāgadhanaṃ? ^0-33

Idha, bhikkhave, ariyasāvako vigatamalamaccharena cetasā agāraṃ ajjhāvasati, ^0-34

Muttacāgo payatapāṇi vossaggarato yācayogo dānasaṃvibhāgarato. ^0-35

Idaṃ vuccati, bhikkhave, cāgadhanaṃ. ^0-36

Katamaṃ ca, bhikkhave, paññādhanaṃ? ^0-37

Idha, bhikkhave, ariyasāvako paññavā hoti, ^0-38

Udayatthagāminiyā paññāya samannāgato ariyāya nibbedhikāya sammā dukkhakkhayagāminiyā. ^0-39

Idaṃ vuccati, bhikkhave, paññādhanaṃ. ^0-40

Imāni kho, bhikkhave, satta dhanānīti. ^0-41

Saddhādhanaṃ sīladhanaṃ hirī ottappiyaṃ dhanaṃ. ^0-42

Sutadhanaṃ ca cāgo ca paññā ve sattamaṃ dhanaṃ. ^0-43

Yassa ete dhanāni atthi, itthiyā purisassa vā. ^0-44

Adaliddhoti taṃ āhu, amoghaṃ tassa jīvitaṃ. ^0-45

Tasmā saddhañca sīlañca, pasādaṃ dhammadassanaṃ. ^0-46

Anuyuñjetha medhāvī, saraṃ buddhāna sāsanaṃti. ^0-47
