# Yasodharātherīapadānaṃ ^0

Ekasmiṃ samaye ramme, iddhe rājagahe pure;
Pabbhāramhi varekamhi, vasante naranāyake. ^1-1

Vasantiyā tamhi nagare, ramme bhikkhunupassaye;
Yasodharābhikkhuniyā, evaṃ āsi vitakkitaṃ. ^1-2

‘‘Suddhodano mahārājā, gotamī ca pajāpatī;
Abhiññātā mahātherā, theriyo ca mahiddhikā. ^1-3

‘‘Santiṃ gatāva āsuṃ te, dīpaccīva nirāsavā;
Lokanāthe dharanteva, ahampi ca sivaṃ padaṃ. ^1-4

‘‘Gamissāmīti cintetvā, passantī āyumattano;
Passitvā āyusaṅkhāraṃ, tadaheva khayaṃ gataṃ. ^1-5

‘‘Pattacīvaramādāya, nikkhamitvā sakassamā;
Purakkhatā bhikkhunībhi, satehi sahassehi sā[^f1]. ^1-6

‘‘Mahiddhikā mahāpaññā, sambuddhaṃ upasaṅkami;
Sambuddhaṃ abhivādetvā, satthuno cakkalakkhaṇe;
Nisinnā ekamantamhi, idaṃ vacanamabravi. ^1-7

‘‘‘Aṭṭhasattativassāhaṃ, pacchimo vattate vayo[^f2];
Pabbhāramhi anuppattā, ārocemi mahāmuni. ^1-8

‘‘‘Paripakko vayo mayhaṃ, parittaṃ mama jīvitaṃ;
Pahāya vo gamissāmi, kataṃ me saraṇamattano. ^1-9

‘‘‘Vayamhi pacchime kāle, maraṇaṃ uparuddhati;
Ajjarattiṃ mahāvīra, pāpuṇissāmi nibbutiṃ. ^1-10

‘‘‘Natthi jāti jarā byādhi, maraṇañca mahāmune;
Ajarāmaraṇaṃ puraṃ, gamissāmi asaṅkhataṃ. ^1-11

‘‘‘Yāvatā parisā nāma, samupāsanti satthuno;
Aparādhamajānantī[^f3], khamantaṃ sammukhā mune. ^1-12

‘‘‘Saṃsaritvā ca saṃsāre, khalitañce mamaṃ tayi;
Ārocemi mahāvīra, aparādhaṃ khamassu me’. ^1-13

‘‘Sutvāna vacanaṃ tassā, munindo idamabravi;
‘Kimuttaraṃ te vakkhāmi, nibbānāya vajantiyā. ^1-14

‘‘‘Iddhiñcāpi nidassehi, mama sāsanakārike;
Parisānañca sabbāsaṃ, kaṅkhaṃ chindassu yāvatā’. ^1-15

‘‘Sutvā taṃ munino vācaṃ, bhikkhunī sā yasodharā;
Vanditvā munirājaṃ taṃ, idaṃ vacanamabravi. ^1-16

‘‘‘Yasodharā ahaṃ vīra, agāre te pajāpati;
Sākiyamhi kule jātā, itthiaṅge patiṭṭhitā. ^1-17

‘‘‘Thīnaṃ satasahassānaṃ, navutīnaṃ chaduttari;
Agāre te ahaṃ vīra, pāmokkhā sabbā issarā. ^1-18

‘‘‘Rūpācāraguṇūpetā, yobbanaṭṭhā piyaṃvadā;
Sabbā maṃ apacāyanti, devatā viya mānusā. ^1-19

‘‘‘Kaññāsatasahassapamukhā, sakyaputtanivesane;
Samānasukhadukkhatā, devatā viya nandane. ^1-20

‘‘‘Kāmadhātumatikkamma[^f4], saṇṭhitā rūpadhātuyā;
Rūpena sadisā natthi, ṭhapetvā lokanāyakaṃ. ^1-21

‘‘‘Sambuddhaṃ abhivādetvā, iddhiṃ dassesi satthuno;
Nekā nānāvidhākārā, mahāiddhīpi dassayī[^f5]. ^1-22

‘‘‘Cakkavāḷasamaṃ kāyaṃ, sīsaṃ uttarato kuru;
Ubho pakkhā duve dīpā, jambudīpaṃ sarīrato. ^1-23

‘‘‘Dakkhiṇañca saraṃ piñchaṃ, nānāsākhā tu pattakā;
Candañca sūriyañcakkhi, merupabbatato sikhaṃ. ^1-24

‘‘‘Cakkavālagiriṃ tuṇḍaṃ, jamburukkhaṃ samūlakaṃ;
Bījamānā upāgantvā, vandantī lokanāyakaṃ. ^1-25

‘‘‘Hatthivaṇṇaṃ tathevassaṃ, pabbataṃ jaladhiṃ tathā;
Candimaṃ sūriyaṃ meruṃ, sakkavaṇṇañca dassayi. ^1-26

‘‘‘Yasodharā ahaṃ vīra, pāde vandāmi cakkhuma;
Sahassalokadhātūnaṃ, phullapadmena chādayi. ^1-27

‘‘‘Brahmavaṇṇañca māpetvā, dhammaṃ desesi suññataṃ;
Yasodharā ahaṃ vīra, pāde vandāmi cakkhuma. ^1-28

‘‘‘Iddhīsu ca vasī homi, dibbāya sotadhātuyā;
Cetopariyañāṇassa, vasī homi mahāmuni. ^1-29

‘‘‘Pubbenivāsaṃ jānāmi, dibbacakkhu visodhitaṃ;
Sabbāsavaparikkhīṇā, natthi dāni punabbhavo. ^1-30

‘‘‘Atthadhammaniruttīsu, paṭibhāne tatheva ca;
Ñāṇaṃ mayhaṃ mahāvīra, uppannaṃ tava santike. ^1-31

‘‘‘Pubbānaṃ lokanāthānaṃ, saṅgamaṃ te nidassitaṃ[^f6];
Adhikāraṃ bahuṃ mayhaṃ, tuyhatthāya mahāmune. ^1-32

‘‘‘Yaṃ mayhaṃ pūritaṃ kammaṃ, kusalaṃ sarase mune;
Tuyhatthāya mahāvīra, puññaṃ upacitaṃ mayā. ^1-33

‘‘‘Abhabbaṭṭhāne vajjetvā, vārayitvā anācaraṃ;
Tuyhatthāya mahāvīra, sañcattaṃ jīvitaṃ mayā. ^1-34

‘‘‘Nekakoṭisahassāni, bhariyatthāyadāsi maṃ;
Na tattha vimanā homi, tuyhatthāya mahāmuni. ^1-35

‘‘‘Nekakoṭisahassāni, upakārāyadāsi maṃ;
Na tattha vimanā homi, tuyhatthāya mahāmuni. ^1-36

‘‘‘Nekakoṭisahassāni, bhojanatthāyadāsi maṃ;
Na tattha vimanā homi, tuyhatthāya mahāmuni. ^1-37

‘‘‘Nekakoṭisahassāni, jīvitāni pariccajiṃ;
Bhayamokkhaṃ karissanti, dadāmi mama jīvitaṃ. ^1-38

‘‘‘Aṅgagate[^f7] alaṅkāre, vatthe nānāvidhe bahū;
Itthimaṇḍe na gūhāmi, tuyhatthāya mahāmuni. ^1-39

‘‘‘Dhanadhaññapariccāgaṃ, gāmāni nigamāni ca;
Khettaṃ puttā ca dhītā ca, pariccattā mahāmuni. ^1-40

‘‘‘Hatthī assā gavā cāpi, dāsiyo paricārikā;
Tuyhatthāya mahāvīra, pariccattā asaṅkhiyā. ^1-41

‘‘‘Yaṃ mayhaṃ paṭimantesi[^f8], dānaṃ dassāmi yācake;
Vimanaṃ me na passāmi, dadato dānamuttamaṃ. ^1-42

‘‘‘Nānāvidhaṃ bahuṃ dukkhaṃ, saṃsāre ca bahubbidhe;
Tuyhatthāya mahāvīra, anubhuttaṃ asaṅkhiyaṃ. ^1-43

‘‘‘Sukhappattānumodāmi, na ca dukkhesu dummanā;
Sabbattha tulitā homi, tuyhatthāya mahāmuni. ^1-44

‘‘‘Anumaggena sambuddho, yaṃ dhammaṃ abhinīhari;
Anubhotvā sukhaṃ dukkhaṃ, patto bodhiṃ mahāmuni. ^1-45

‘‘‘Brahmadevañca sambuddhaṃ, gotamaṃ lokanāyakaṃ;
Aññesaṃ lokanāthānaṃ, saṅgamaṃ te bahuṃ mayā. ^1-46

‘‘‘Adhikāraṃ bahuṃ mayhaṃ, tuyhatthāya mahāmuni;
Gavesato buddhadhamme, ahaṃ te paricārikā. ^1-47

‘‘‘Kappe ca satasahasse, caturo ca asaṅkhiye;
Dīpaṅkaro mahāvīro, uppajji lokanāyako. ^1-48

‘‘‘Paccantadesavisaye, nimantetvā tathāgataṃ;
Tassa āgamanaṃ maggaṃ, sodhenti tuṭṭhamānasā. ^1-49

‘‘‘Tena kālena so āsi, sumedho nāma brāhmaṇo;
Maggañca paṭiyādesi, āyato[^f9] sabbadassino. ^1-50

‘‘‘Tena kālenahaṃ āsiṃ, kaññā brāhmaṇasambhavā;
Sumittānāma nāmena, upagacchiṃ samāgamaṃ. ^1-51

‘‘‘Aṭṭha uppalahatthāni, pūjanatthāya satthuno;
Ādāya janasaṃmajjhe, addasaṃ isi muggataṃ. ^1-52

‘‘‘Cirānugataṃ dayitaṃ[^f10], atikkantaṃ manoharaṃ;
Disvā tadā amaññissaṃ, saphalaṃ jīvitaṃ mama. ^1-53

‘‘‘Parakkamaṃ taṃ saphalaṃ, addasaṃ isino tadā;
Pubbakammena sambuddhe, cittañcāpi pasīdi me. ^1-54

‘‘‘Bhiyyo cittaṃ pasādesiṃ, ise uggatamānase;
Deyyaṃ aññaṃ na passāmi, demi pupphāni te isi. ^1-55

‘‘‘Pañcahatthā tava hontu, tayo hontu mamaṃ ise;
Tena saddhiṃ samā hontu, bodhatthāya tavaṃ ise’. ^1-56

Catutthaṃ bhāṇavāraṃ. ^a-1

‘‘Isi gahetvā pupphāni, āgacchantaṃ mahāyasaṃ;
Pūjesi janasaṃmajjhe, bodhatthāya mahāisi. ^1-57

‘‘Passitvā janasaṃmajjhe, dīpaṅkaro mahāmuni;
Viyākāsi mahāvīro, isi muggatamānasaṃ. ^1-58

‘‘Aparimeyye ito kappe, dīpaṅkaro mahāmuni;
Mama kammaṃ viyākāsi, ujubhāvaṃ mahāmuni. ^1-59

‘‘‘Samacittā samakammā, samakārī bhavissati;
Piyā hessati kammena, tuyhatthāya mahāisi[^f11]. ^1-60

‘‘‘Sudassanā supiyā ca, manāpā piyavādinī;
Tassa dhammesu dāyādā, viharissati iddhikā. ^1-61

‘‘‘Yathāpi bhaṇḍasāmuggaṃ, anurakkhati sāmino;
Evaṃ kusaladhammānaṃ, anurakkhissate ayaṃ. ^1-62

‘‘‘Tassa te[^f12] anukampantī, pūrayissati pāramī;
Sīhova pañjaraṃ bhetvā[^f13], pāpuṇissati bodhiyaṃ’. ^1-63

‘‘Aparimeyye ito kappe, yaṃ maṃ buddho viyākarī;
Taṃ vācaṃ anumodentī, evaṃkārī bhaviṃ ahaṃ. ^1-64

‘‘Tassa kammassa sukatassa, tattha cittaṃ pasādayiṃ;
Devamanussakaṃ yoniṃ, anubhotvā asaṅkhiyaṃ. ^1-65

‘‘Sukhadukkhenubhotvāhaṃ, devesu mānusesu ca;
Pacchime bhave sampatte, ajāyiṃ sākiye kule. ^1-66

‘‘Rūpavatī bhogavatī, yasasīlavatī tato;
Sabbaṅgasampadā homi, kulesu abhisakkatā. ^1-67

‘‘Lābhaṃ silokaṃ sakkāraṃ, lokadhammasamāgamaṃ;
Cittañca dukkhitaṃ natthi, vasāmi akutobhayā. ^1-68

‘‘Vuttañhetaṃ bhagavatā, rañño antepure tadā;
Khattiyānaṃ pure vīra, upakārañca niddisi. ^1-69

‘‘Upakārā ca yā nārī, yā ca nārī sukhe dukhe;
Atthakkhāyī ca yā nārī, yā ca nārīnukampikā. ^1-70

‘‘Pañcakoṭisatā buddhā, navakoṭisatāni ca;
Etesaṃ devadevānaṃ, mahādānaṃ pavattayiṃ. ^1-71

‘‘Adhikāraṃ mahā[^f14] mayhaṃ, dhammarāja suṇohi me;
Ekādasakoṭisatā, buddhā dvādasa koṭiyo[^f15]. ^1-72

‘‘Etesaṃ devadevānaṃ, mahādānaṃ pavattayiṃ;
Adhikāraṃ mahā mayhaṃ, dhammarāja suṇohi me. ^1-73

‘‘‘Vīsakoṭisatā buddhā, tiṃsakoṭisatāni ca;
Etesaṃ devadevānaṃ, mahādānaṃ pavattayiṃ. ^1-74

‘‘‘Adhikāraṃ mahā mayhaṃ, dhammarāja suṇohi me;
Cattālīsakoṭisatā, paññāsa koṭisatāni ca. ^1-75

‘‘‘Etesaṃ devadevānaṃ, mahādānaṃ pavattayiṃ;
Adhikāraṃ mahā mayhaṃ, dhammarāja suṇohi me. ^1-76

‘‘‘Saṭṭhikoṭisatā buddhā, sattatikoṭisatāni ca;
Etesaṃ devadevānaṃ, mahādānaṃ pavattayiṃ. ^1-77

‘‘‘Adhikāraṃ mahā mayhaṃ, dhammarāja suṇohi me;
Asītikoṭisatā buddhā, navutikoṭisatāni ca. ^1-78

‘‘‘Etesaṃ devadevānaṃ, mahādānaṃ pavattayiṃ;
Adhikāraṃ mahā mayhaṃ, dhammarāja suṇohi me. ^1-79

‘‘‘Koṭisatasahassāni, honti lokagganāyakā;
Etesaṃ devadevānaṃ, mahādānaṃ pavattayiṃ. ^1-80

‘‘‘Adhikāraṃ mahā mayhaṃ, dhammarāja suṇohi me;
Navakoṭisahassāni, apare lokanāyakā. ^1-81

‘‘‘Etesaṃ devadevānaṃ, mahādānaṃ pavattayiṃ;
Adhikāraṃ mahā mayhaṃ, dhammarāja suṇohi me. ^1-82

‘‘‘Koṭisatasahassāni, pañcāsītimahesinaṃ;
Pañcāsītikoṭisatā, sattatiṃsā ca koṭiyo. ^1-83

‘‘‘Etesaṃ devadevānaṃ, mahādānaṃ pavattayiṃ;
Adhikāraṃ mahā mayhaṃ, dhammarāja suṇohi me. ^1-84

‘‘‘Paccekabuddhā vītarāgā[^f16], aṭṭhaṭṭhamakakoṭiyo[^f17];
Adhikāraṃ mahā mayhaṃ, dhammarāja suṇohi me. ^1-85

‘‘‘Khīṇāsavā vītamalā, asaṅkhiyā buddhasāvakā;
Adhikāraṃ mahā mayhaṃ, dhammarāja suṇohi me. ^1-86

‘‘‘Evaṃ dhamme suciṇṇānaṃ, sadā dhammassa cārinaṃ;
Dhammacārī sukhaṃ seti, asmiṃ loke paramhi ca. ^1-87

‘‘‘Dhammaṃ care sucaritaṃ, na naṃ duccaritaṃ care;
Dhammacārī sukhaṃ seti, asmiṃ loke paramhi ca. ^1-88

‘‘‘Nibbinditvāna saṃsāre, pabbajiṃ anagāriyaṃ;
Sahassaparivārena, pabbajitvā akiñcanā. ^1-89

‘‘‘Agāraṃ vijahitvāna, pabbajiṃ anagāriyaṃ;
Aḍḍhamāse asampatte, catusaccamapāpuṇiṃ. ^1-90

‘‘‘Cīvaraṃ piṇḍapātañca, paccayaṃ sayanāsanaṃ;
Upanenti bahū janā, sāgareyeva ūmiyo. ^1-91

‘‘‘Kilesā jhāpitā mayhaṃ…pe… viharāmi anāsavā. ^1-92

‘‘‘Svāgataṃ vata me āsi…pe… kataṃ buddhassa sāsanaṃ. ^1-93

‘‘‘Paṭisambhidā catasso…pe… kataṃ buddhassa sāsanaṃ’. ^1-94

‘‘Evaṃ bahuvidhaṃ dukkhaṃ, sampattī ca bahubbidhā;
Visuddhibhāvaṃ sampattā, labhāmi sabbasampadā. ^1-95

‘‘Yā dadāti sakattānaṃ, puññatthāya mahesino;
Sahāyasampadā honti, nibbānapadamasaṅkhataṃ. ^1-96

‘‘Parikkhīṇaṃ atītañca, paccuppannaṃ anāgataṃ;
Sabbakammaṃ mamaṃ khīṇaṃ, pāde vandāmi cakkhuma’’. ^1-97

Itthaṃ sudaṃ yasodharā bhikkhunī bhagavato sammukhā imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti. ^a-2

Yasodharātheriyāpadānaṃ aṭṭhamaṃ. ^a-3
