# Vasala Sutta ^0

“Evaṃ me sutaṃ,” ^0-1

“Ekaṃ samayaṃ Bhagavā Sāvatthiyaṃ viharati Jetavane Anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme.” ^0-2

“Atha kho Bhagavā pubbaṇhasamayaṃ nivāsetvā pattacīvaramādāya Sāvatthiyaṃ piṇḍāya pāvisi.” ^0-3

“Tena kho pana samaye Aggikabhāradvājassa brāhmaṇassa nivesane aggi pajjalito hoti āhuti paggahitā.” ^0-4

“Atha kho Bhagavā Sāvatthiyaṃ sapadānaṃ piṇḍāya caramāno yena Aggikabhāradvājassa brāhmaṇassa nivesanaṃ tenupasaṅkami.” ^0-5

“Addasā kho Aggikabhāradvājo brāhmaṇo Bhagavantaṃ dūratova āgacchantaṃ,” ^0-6

“Disvāna Bhagavantaṃ etadavoca—” ^0-7

“Tatreva muṇḍaka, tatreva samaṇaka, tatreva vasalaka, tiṭṭhāhīti.” ^0-8

“Evaṃ vutte, Bhagavā Aggikabhāradvājaṃ brāhmaṇaṃ etadavoca—” ^0-9

“Jānāsi pana tvaṃ, brāhmaṇa, vasalaṃ vā vasalakaraṇe vā dhamme?” ^0-10

“Na khvāhaṃ, bho Gotama, jānāmi vasalaṃ vā vasalakaraṇe vā dhamme.” ^0-11

“Sādhu me bhavaṃ Gotamo tathā dhammaṃ desetu yathāhaṃ jāneyyaṃ vasalaṃ vā vasalakaraṇe vā dhammeti.” ^0-12

“Tena hi, brāhmaṇa, suṇāhi,” ^0-13

“Sādhukaṃ manasikarohi, bhāsissāmīti.” ^0-14

“Evamiti kho Aggikabhāradvājo brāhmaṇo Bhagavato paccassosi.” ^0-15

“Bhagavā etadavoca—” ^0-16

“Kodhano upanāhī ca, pāpamakkhī ca yo naro.” ^0-17

“Vippatiṭṭhī māyāvī, taṃ jaññā vasalo iti.” ^0-18

“Ekajaṃ vā dijaṃ vā pi, yo idha pāṇāni hiṃsati.” ^0-19

“Yassa pāṇe dayā natthi, taṃ jaññā vasalo iti.” ^0-20

“Yo hanti parisaṅgāni, gāmāni nigamāni ca.” ^0-21

“Nigāhako samaññāto, taṃ jaññā vasalo iti.” ^0-22

“Gāme vā yadi vāraññe, yaṃ paresaṃ samāyitaṃ.” ^0-23

“Theyyā adinnamādiyati, taṃ jaññā vasalo iti.” ^0-24

“Yo have iṇamādāya, cujjamāno palāyati.” ^0-25

“Na hi te iṇam-atthīti, taṃ jaññā vasalo iti.” ^0-26

“Yo ve kiñcikkhakkhakāmatāya, panthasmiṃ vajataṃ janaṃ.” ^0-27

“Hantvā kiñcikkhamādeti, taṃ jaññā vasalo iti.” ^0-28

“Yo atthahetu pareheti, dhanahetu ca yo naro.” ^0-29

“Sakkhipuṭṭho musā brūti, taṃ jaññā vasalo iti.” ^0-30

“Yo jātīnaṃ sakhānaṃ vā, dāresu patidissati.” ^0-31

“Sahasā sāpiyena vā, taṃ jaññā vasalo iti.” ^0-32

“Yo mātaraṃ vā pitaraṃ vā, jiṇṇakaṃ gatayobbanaṃ.” ^0-33

“Pahaṃsato na bharati, taṃ jaññā vasalo iti.” ^0-34

“Yo mātaraṃ vā pitaraṃ vā, bhātaraṃ bhaginiṃ sasuṃ.” ^0-35

“Hanati ros eti vācāya, taṃ jaññā vasalo iti.” ^0-36

“Yo atthaṃ pucchito santo, anatthamanusāsati.” ^0-37

“Paṭicchannaṃ manteti, taṃ jaññā vasalo iti.” ^0-38

“Yo katvā pāpakaṃ kammaṃ, ‘mā maṃ jaññā’ti icchati.” ^0-39

“Yo paṭicchannakammanto, taṃ jaññā vasalo iti.” ^0-40

“Yo ve parakule gantvā, bhutvāna suci bhojanaṃ.” ^0-41

“Āgataṃ na paṭipūjeti, taṃ jaññā vasalo iti.” ^0-42

“Yo brāhmaṇaṃ vā samaṇaṃ vā, aññaṃ vā pi vaṇijjakaṃ.” ^0-43

“Musāvādena vañceti, taṃ jaññā vasalo iti.” ^0-44

“Yo brāhmaṇaṃ vā samaṇaṃ vā, bhattakāle upaṭṭhitaṃ.” ^0-45

“Roseti vācā na ca deti, taṃ jaññā vasalo iti.” ^0-46

“Asantaṃ yodha pavati, mohena paṭilīḷaggo.” ^0-47

“Kiñcikkhaṃ nijigiṃsāno, taṃ jaññā vasalo iti.” ^0-48

Yo cattānaṃ samukkaṃse, paraṃ ca avajānāti. ^0-49

Nihīno sena mānena, taṃ jaññā vasalo iti. ^0-50

Rosako kadariyo ca, pāpiccho maccharī saṭho. ^0-51

Ahiriko anoṭṭāpī, taṃ jaññā vasalo iti. ^0-52

Rosako kadariyo ca, pāpiccho maccharī saṭho. ^0-53

Ahiriko anoṭṭāpī, taṃ jaññā vasalo iti. ^0-54

Yo Buddhaṃ paribhāsati, atha vā tassa sāvakaṃ. ^0-55

Paribbājaṃ gahaṃ vā, taṃ jaññā vasalo iti. ^0-56

Yo ve anarahā santo, arahaṃ paṭijānāti. ^0-57

Coro sabbassa loke, esa kho vasalādhamo. ^0-58

Na jāti vasalo hoti. ^0-59

Na jaccā vasalo hoti, na jaccā hoti brāhmaṇo. ^0-60

Kammunā vasalo hoti, kammunā hoti brāhmaṇo. ^0-61

Tavam iminā pi viññeyya, yathā medaṃ nidassanaṃ. ^0-62

Caṇḍālaputto Sopāko, Mātaṅgo iti vissuto. ^0-63

So yasaṃ paramaṃ patto, Mātaṅgo yaṃ sudullabhaṃ. ^0-64

Ārādhayitvā khattiye ca, brāhmaṇe bahū. ^0-65

So devayānaṃ ārūḷha, virajaṃ so mahāpathaṃ. ^0-66

Kāmarāgaṃ virājetvā, brahmalokūpago ahu. ^0-67

Na naṃ jāti nivāresi, brahmalokūpapattiyā. ^0-68

Ajjhāyakā kule jātā, brāhmaṇā mantabandhuno. ^0-69

Te ca pāpesu kammesu, abhiṇham upadissare. ^0-70

Diṭṭhe va dhamme gārayhā, samparāye ca duggatiṃ. ^0-71

Na te jāti nivāresi, duggaccā garahāya vā. ^0-72

Na jaccā vasalo hoti, na jaccā hoti brāhmaṇo. ^0-73

Kammunā vasalo hoti, kammunā hoti brāhmaṇo. ^0-74

## Aggikabhāradvājassa saraṇāgamanaṃ ^1-0

Evaṃ vutte Aggikabhāradvājo brāhmaṇo Bhagavantaṃ etadavoca— ^1-1

“Abhikkantaṃ bho Gotama ...” ^1-2

“Esāhaṃ Bhagavantaṃ Gotamaṃ saraṇaṃ gacchāmi, Dhammañca bhikkhusaṅghañca.” ^1-3

“Upāsakaṃ maṃ bhavaṃ Gotamo dhāretu ajjatagge pāṇupetaṃ saraṇaṃ gatanti.” ^1-4
